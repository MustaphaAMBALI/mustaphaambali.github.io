About Me

I turn transaction data into profit decisions.

Most e-commerce brands know their revenue number. Very few know where their profit actually goes after returns, platform fees, discounts, and acquisition costs are stripped away. That gap is where I work.

My background is in commercial analytics — building the kind of analysis that changes a budget meeting, not just decorates a slide deck. I combine SQL-driven data modelling, Python for data engineering, and Power BI dashboards that business teams can actually use.

Based in Lagos, Nigeria and open to remote roles globally across e-commerce, retail, sales analytics, and commercial intelligence.

E-commerce Revenue & Profitability
ProfitLens

24-month profitability audit for a DTC fashion brand doing $2.2M in GMV. Decomposed every dollar from gross revenue to contribution margin across three channels and four product categories.

Key finding: Amazon generated 29% of GMV but delivered −6.47% contribution margin. TikTok Shop drove 65% of total profit.

LoyaltyLens

Customer retention and LTV audit for a DTC beauty brand spending 40% more on ads with flat revenue. 24-month cohort analysis across 5,000 customers and 5 acquisition channels.

RevRadar

End-to-end automated sales monitoring system across 2,539 deal records for 10 reps in 4 regions. Daily automated alerts delivered to management via n8n, eliminating manual month-end reviews.
